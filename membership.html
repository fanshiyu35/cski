Membership Grades

Membership Structure

ČSKI maintains a three-tier membership structure designed to recognise the full spectrum of professional achievement in cybernetics, informatics, and related fields. Membership is governed by the Society's Stanovy (Statutes), as adopted by the General Assembly and registered with the Municipal Court in Prague.

Regular Member (Řádný člen)

Open to individuals holding a university degree in cybernetics, informatics, computer science, mathematics, or a related discipline, or demonstrating equivalent professional experience. Regular Members have the right to attend General Assembly meetings, vote on Society matters, and participate in all Society activities.

Annual dues: 800 CZK

Senior Member (Významný člen)

Senior membership is conferred upon Regular Members who have demonstrated at least ten years of sustained professional contribution to the field and have been active in Society affairs. Candidates for Senior Member must be nominated by at least two existing Senior Members or Fellows, and their nomination reviewed by the Membership Committee.

Annual dues: 1,200 CZK

Fellow (Čestný člen — Fellow)

The grade of Fellow is the highest distinction conferred by ČSKI. It is reserved for individuals who have made outstanding achievements in cybernetics, informatics, or closely related fields, and whose body of work has exerted a significant and lasting influence on research, education, or practice.

Election Criteria (Stanovy §4.3)

In accordance with Section 4.3 of the Society's Stanovy, election to the Fellow grade requires:

  1. Nomination — The candidate must be nominated by at least two existing Fellows of the Society. The nomination shall be accompanied by a detailed statement of the candidate's contributions and their significance to the field.
  2. Demonstrated Outstanding Achievements — The candidate must have demonstrated outstanding achievements in cybernetics, informatics, or a related discipline. Such achievements may be evidenced by, inter alia, publications in leading scientific venues, sustained contributions to theory or methodology, recognised impact on industrial practice, or leadership in the scientific community.
  3. Fellow Committee Review — Nominations are reviewed by the Fellow Committee (Komise pro čestné členství), which evaluates each candidate's qualifications and makes a recommendation to the General Assembly.
  4. Election by the General Assembly — The General Assembly votes on the Fellow Committee's recommendations. A two-thirds majority of members present is required for election.

The total number of living Fellows shall not exceed twenty at any given time, ensuring that the Fellow grade remains a mark of true distinction within the Czech and international cybernetics and informatics community.

Annual dues: 1,500 CZK
