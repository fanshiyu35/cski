About the Society

History and Mission

The Czech Society for Cybernetics and Informatics (ČSKI) was founded on 26 September 1990, shortly after the Velvet Revolution, bringing together researchers from the former Institute of Information Theory and Automation (ÚTIA) of the Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences and faculty from Charles University and the Czech Technical University in Prague.

The mission of the Society is to contribute to the advancement of cybernetics, informatics, and related disciplines within the Czech Republic — encompassing conceptual, scientific, and pedagogical activities, as well as the dissemination and practical application of research results.

IFIP Membership

ČSKI has been the national member representing the Czech Republic in the International Federation for Information Processing (IFIP) since its founding. IFIP, established in 1960 under the auspices of UNESCO, links over 50 national and international societies and academies of science, with a total membership exceeding half a million professionals worldwide.

Through IFIP's 13 Technical Committees, ČSKI members participate in global working groups spanning artificial intelligence, software engineering, security, human-computer interaction, and theoretical computer science.

Legal Status

ČSKI is registered as a spolek (association) under Czech law, in accordance with Act No. 89/2012 Sb. (Civil Code). The Society is recorded in the Spolkový rejstřík (Register of Associations) maintained by the Municipal Court in Prague.

Statutory Body

The Society is represented by its Chair (předseda), elected by the General Assembly. The Chair is supported by a three-member Audit Committee (revizní komise) that oversees the Society's compliance with its Stanovy (Statutes) and applicable law.
