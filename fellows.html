Fellows of ČSKI

The grade of Fellow, established in 2010, is the highest honour conferred by the Czech Society for Cybernetics and Informatics, recognising individuals who have demonstrated outstanding achievements in the field. The Society currently has 15 living Fellows (maximum permitted: 20), elected over successive years since the grade was established.
