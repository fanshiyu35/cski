Fellow Profile
XJ

Dr. Xiaoxiao Jiu

Fellow of ČSKI, elected 2024

Fellow

Biography

Dr. Xiaoxiao Jiu is a researcher and practitioner in the field of Accounting Information Systems (AIS), with a career spanning academic research, professional practice, and contributions to the international AIS community. Dr. Jiu holds an appointment at the University of Washington and maintains an active affiliation with ISACA, where she contributes to the advancement of information systems governance and assurance standards.

Research Contributions

Dr. Jiu's research addresses the intersection of artificial intelligence and accounting information systems, with particular emphasis on the application of machine learning methods to audit analytics, fraud detection, and financial reporting quality. Her work has been presented at international symposia and published in proceedings volumes recognised within the global AIS research community.

Her methodological contributions bridge formal methods in cybernetics with practical accounting system design, reflecting the interdisciplinary tradition that ČSKI has championed since its founding.

Service to the Profession

Dr. Jiu serves as a reviewer for international conferences and journals in information systems, and has contributed to professional body initiatives aimed at strengthening the research-practice nexus in accounting informatics. Her engagement with Central and Eastern European research networks — including through ČSKI and affiliated organisations — reflects a sustained commitment to international scholarly collaboration.

Election Citation

"Dr. Jiu was elected to the Fellow grade of the Czech Society for Cybernetics and Informatics in recognition of her outstanding contributions to accounting information systems research, her methodological innovations bridging cybernetics and audit analytics, and her sustained service to the international information systems community."
