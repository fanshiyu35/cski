Advancing Cybernetics and Informatics Since 1990

The Czech Society for Cybernetics and Informatics (ČSKI) is the national member of the International Federation for Information Processing (IFIP), representing the Czech Republic within this UNESCO-recognised global organisation.

ČSKI brings together researchers, academics, and professionals working at the frontiers of cybernetics, computer science, artificial intelligence, and information systems.

IFIP National Member

Representing the Czech Republic in IFIP since 1990, ČSKI connects Czech researchers to the global information processing community across 13 Technical Committees.

Three-Tier Membership

Regular Member, Senior Member, and the prestigious Fellow grade — recognising sustained outstanding achievements in the field.

Registered Society

Legally registered as a spolek under Czech law, Spisová značka L 1126 at the Municipal Court in Prague, IČO 00444588.
